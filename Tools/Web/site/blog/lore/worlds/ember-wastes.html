Circle X · Floors 1000-901

The Ember Wastes

Lava rivers where the portal spits out everything that falls through. The bottom of Hell, and the first hundred floors of the climb.

BiomeLava rivers, slag, falling embers
FamilyImps and cinders
WardenThe Magma King, Floor 901
Stages10, ten floors each

The stages

Each stage is a place with one beat. One new thing at a time, introduced alone, never more than three enemies on screen.

FloorsPlaceWhat happens
1000-991The LandingThe lava river where the portal spits everything out. One imp, alone, sees the hero and drops its ember in surprise.
990-981The SpillThe village's debris, the village sign and the first cinder. "That was the baker's cart."
980-971Imp RunImps hauling the diamond sacks down, under a carved IMPS ONLY. Diamonds pour.
970-961The Slag FallsGlowing falls, and slag dripping from above (MIND THE DRIP). "It's raining rock. Hot rock."
960-951The LoopThe same rock, the same crack, YOU ARE HERE. AGAIN., and Floor 957 twice. "It's doing that on purpose."
950-941Halfway BasinHalfway to 900 (HALFWAY! (TO 900)). Thatch cinders, then the first real mixed fights.
940-931The CrustA skin of cooling slag on the river that sinks if you stop (DON'T STOP), and embers raining from the steam.
930-921The Sack PileThe imps' camp (KEEP OFF THE PILE) and the Magma King's guard. Clear the line and the pile spills.
920-911The Heat StairsThe approach to the throne (TO THE THRONE), and his voice coming down the stairs. Ends at his door.
910-901The Slag ThroneThe Magma King's arena. Beat him and the Waystone at 901 lights.

Why it's like this

Everything that falls through the portal lands here: the village's diamonds in the imps' sacks, the village's debris, the village's sparks. The imps carry the diamonds up toward the Overlord's gate; the Magma King takes his cut on the way. Above it all, the falls drip, the river steams, and the floors get hotter the closer you get to 901.

See also: imps, cinders, the Wastes' hazards.
