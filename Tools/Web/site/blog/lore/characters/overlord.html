Floor 1 · Final Boss

The Overlord

Ruler of all 1,000 floors of Hell, and as sick of it as the hero is of everything. He wants the surface. He's taking his time about it.

WhatA giant obsidian titan
EyesRed
CrownBlack horns
RulesHell, all 1,000 floors
SitsFloor 1, under the meadow
HurryNone

What he wants

Hell is dark, loud and the same forever, and the Overlord is done waiting. He wants the surface. Diamonds are what holds a portal open, so he came up on a nice afternoon, had his imps steal the village's diamonds, burned the meadow empty, and stepped back down to gather his army.

Now he sits at the top of Hell, Floor 1, right under the meadow, feeding the diamonds into a gate that will tear Hell open onto the surface for good. The hero has 999 floors to stop him.

How he talks

Very little. Deep, slow, enormous, barely bothered: a voice that fills a valley without being raised. Short lines, long pauses. Every word sounds like it cost him nothing.

The Overlord, burning a hole beside the heroMm. ... Stay.

He barely notices the hero. That will change, eventually. He can't ignore someone who keeps coming back.

What's waiting at Floor 1

The Throne Stair: his palace and his half-built gate, daylight leaking down the stairs, and his personal guard. What happens when the hero gets there, Hell hasn't shown anyone yet.

What changed. The old page here said he burns villages for no reason. He has a reason: the surface, and the diamonds that open the way to it. He's still not nice about it.
