1. Open the beta site's game, Play, and pick Floor 998 ("The Spill") from the level select (or climb there from the Landing).
2. Play it: the river and the cinder come first, then five named set pieces (THE HAY WAKES, EMBER RAIN, SPARKS FROM THE SIGN, THE JAM, THE SPIRE).
3. Notice the screen filling up: each piece throws more fire than the one before; hold R to rewind when hit.
4. It takes about 4.5-5 minutes to reach the landing, then the Grudge pick.
