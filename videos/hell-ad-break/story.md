Logline: Hell runs a cable-TV ad for its own 1,000 floors, with a slime sponsor hawking diamonds, and the deadpan hero, the only one actually stuck in it, is not buying.
Announcer: wants you to buy the climb; booming, delighted. Yooba: wants to sell his diamonds; fast, wet, too pleased with himself. Hero: wants it to stop; flat, unimpressed.
Turn: the hero cuts in: "You're selling me the hole I'm stuck in." Payoff: the announcer and Yooba take that as a yes, and the hero's long beat ends on "...Great."
Why it matters: Video3 after the Landing: Hell is a show that sells itself, Yooba is a friendly villager and the hero's attitude is the game's joke (Docs/Story.md, Video3 is Hell's Ad Break).
Who it's for: anyone who has seen a shopping-channel ad; no knowledge of the game needed.
